Implement `/app/solver.py`, a program that designs and runs a follow-up single-cell RNA study and reports which genes respond to a treatment in each of three cell types.

A pilot of four donors is already sequenced: two control donors in batch 1 and two case donors in batch 2. Gene expression differs between donors and between batches, and the treatment changes a few of the 25 test genes (G16 to G40) in one cell type only. Cell types are not labelled; the 15 marker genes (G01 to G15, five per type) identify them and are not treatment-responsive.

Your program talks to the lab over standard input and standard output with newline-delimited JSON, through `labclient.py`. With a budget of 130 cost units you can recruit new donors (case or control) into a chosen batch with a chosen number of cells, prepare another library from a donor you already have, and sequence or resequence a library to a chosen depth. Each batch holds at most 4 libraries. A new donor adds biology and a library adds cells from that donor; resequencing a library adds reads for the same cells and never adds cells or donors. `counts` returns a library's current cell-by-gene counts and QC and is free; `status` reads the ledger. Nothing is returned for libraries you do not sequence.

Submit, for each of the three cell types and each of the 25 test genes (75 tests), `log2fc` (case versus control), a 95% interval `ci` and `significant` (your decision at an FDR of 5%). Also submit a `ledger` of the budget spent and the sequenced libraries, which must match the session record, and a `follow_up` recommendation: the next step (`recruit_donors`, `new_libraries`, `resequence` or `no_further_acquisition`), the cell type it targets (or null) and a written rationale. The follow-up is checked for form only; its content is not graded.

A result is accepted for a case when, across the 75 tests, at least 5 of the 8 truly responding gene-in-type pairs are called significant with the right sign, no more than 2 non-responding pairs are called significant, all 75 tests are reported with finite values and the lower end of each interval not above its upper end, and every test called significant has an interval that excludes 0. Intervals are checked for these validity conditions only; their coverage and width are reported in the practice output but not graded. At least 6 of the 8 held-out cases must be accepted; the margin allows for the sampling variation a correct analysis still shows. Cases differ in donors, batch effects, cell-type proportions, library quality and which genes respond.

`/app/SPEC.md` has the protocol and exact model. `python3 /app/practice.py` runs your `solver.py` on a disclosed practice case whose true parameters are in `/app/practice_case.json`.
